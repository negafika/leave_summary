Leave Summary

Clear visibility into leave balances, accrual expiry, and historical leave data.

Leave Summary extends Odoo 19 Time Off with focused reporting and expiry history, helping HR users understand where leave stands today and what has expired over time.

Know Where Your Leave Stands

Leave balances can become difficult to understand when accruals, carryovers, leave taken, and expiry are spread across different records.

Leave Summary brings the information together into a clearer reporting experience for HR teams and Odoo users.

See total entitlement, allocated leave, leave taken, remaining balance, and expired accrual leave in a single view.

Designed around Odoo's native Time Off.

Odoo remains responsible for accrual calculation, carryover, validity, and expiry. Leave Summary adds visibility and historical analysis around that process.

Key Features

  • Leave Summary — View entitlement, allocations, leave taken, remaining balance, and expired leave together.
  • Expiry History — Review recorded accrual expiry events by employee, leave type, expiry date, and expired days.
  • Allocation Traceability — Follow an expiry record back to its originating accrual allocation.
  • As-of-Date Reporting — Examine leave information from a selected historical date rather than only today's position.
  • Expiry Analysis — Identify expired leave and review expiry information using Odoo's filtering, grouping, list, and pivot views.
  • Native Odoo Integration — Works with the standard Time Off accrual configuration instead of introducing a separate accrual or expiry engine.

Leave Summary

One place for the numbers that matter

The Leave Summary report gives HR users a consolidated view of employee leave balances.

Review the relationship between entitlement, allocation, consumption, remaining balance, and expired accrual leave without moving between multiple Time Off records.

Historical reporting can also be generated using an as-of date, making it easier to examine the leave position at a particular point in time.

Expiry History

See what expired, when, and where it came from

Leave Summary keeps a dedicated history of relevant accrual expiry events, giving HR users a much clearer way to investigate expired leave.

Each record provides the employee, leave type, originating allocation, carryover date, expiry date, and number of expired days.

Expiry records can be filtered and grouped directly from the familiar Odoo reporting interface.

Built Around Odoo's Native Accrual Logic

Leave Summary is an audit and reporting layer — not a replacement for Odoo's Time Off engine.

Odoo calculates accruals

Accrual balances and allocation processing remain part of Odoo's standard Time Off functionality.

Odoo applies configured rules

Carryover, validity, and expiry continue to follow the configuration of the native accrual plan.

Leave Summary records history

Relevant expiry events are recorded for historical review and reporting.

Your Odoo configuration remains authoritative

The module does not introduce a parallel set of accrual or expiry rules.

What Leave Summary Does Not Change

  • It does not replace Odoo's accrual calculation.
  • It does not implement a separate expiry engine.
  • It does not override native leave consumption.
  • It does not introduce separate expiry rules.
  • It does not replace the configured Odoo accrual plan.

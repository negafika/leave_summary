# Leave Summary

Clear visibility and reporting for employee leave balances and accrual expiry in Odoo 19.

## Purpose

**Leave Summary** helps HR teams easily understand employee leave balances and keep track of leave that has expired.

It works alongside Odoo's standard Time Off and Accrual Plans, adding a clear history of expired leave and convenient summary reports.

## Features

* **Expiry History / Analysis** — See when leave expired, for which employee, the type of leave, and the amount that expired.
* **Leave Summary** — Get a clear overview of allocated, taken, remaining, and expired leave.
* **Automatic Expiry Tracking** — Expired leave is automatically recorded when Odoo processes it.
* **Allocation History** — Quickly view expiry history related to a specific leave allocation.
* **Historical Reporting** — Review leave balances and expiry information as of a selected date.
* **List and Pivot Views** — Easily filter, group, and analyze expiry information using Odoo's standard reporting tools.

## Configuration

The module works with Odoo's standard **Accrual Plans**.

Simply configure your accrual plan and its validity period as usual:

1. Go to **Time Off → Configuration → Accrual Plans**.
2. Open the relevant accrual plan.
3. Configure the **Carry over** option.
4. Enable **Accrual Validity** and set the validity period.

The module automatically keeps a record of leave that expires according to these settings.

## Reporting

### Expiry History / Analysis

Review expired leave with information such as:

* Employee
* Department
* Time Off Type
* Expiry Date
* Expired Days

Use list and pivot views to filter, group, and analyze expiry information.

### Leave Summary

Get a consolidated overview of employee leave, including:

* Allocated Days
* Taken Days
* Remaining Days
* Expired Days
* Closest Expiry
* Next Expiry Date

The report can be viewed **as of a selected date**, making it easier to review historical leave information.

## Works With Odoo Time Off

Leave Summary is designed to work alongside Odoo's standard Time Off features. Your existing accrual plans and leave settings remain unchanged.

The module focuses on providing **better visibility, history, and reporting** so HR teams can easily understand what happened to employee leave over time.
